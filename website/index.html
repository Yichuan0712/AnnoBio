Use Cases

Structured prompt engineering with datasets and evaluation fits many domains. Build, run, score, and iterate, whether you work in research or production.

Medicine
Medical Q&A, clinical note summarization, diagnosis support. Ground prompts on guidelines and evaluate against expert answers.
Biology
Literature summarization, experiment data extraction, gene/protein analysis. Iterate prompts on curated scientific datasets.
Legal
Contract review, clause extraction, legal research. Map outputs to ground truth and refine prompts for accuracy.
Finance
Report analysis, risk assessment, compliance checks. Run on sample data, score against expected outputs, iterate.
Education
Grading, tutoring, curriculum generation. Build datasets of questions and ideal answers; tune prompts for consistency.
Customer Service
FAQ extraction, ticket classification, knowledge-base QA. Use Run, Score, and Review to improve support automation.
Research
Literature review, hypothesis generation, structured data extraction. Version prompts and compare models on benchmarks.
